# Labyrinth Foundation

Labyrinth Foundation Inc. is a 501(c)(3) public charity creating free educational and creative games, interactive experiences, and digital projects for learning through play.

**Website:** https://labyrinthfoundation.org  
**EIN:** 39-3464420

## Our work

We use GitHub to develop and maintain the software behind our nonprofit programs, including our collection of free games and interactive experiences, our website, and supporting infrastructure.

Our projects support Labyrinth Foundation's charitable mission and are made available to their audiences without charge.

### Games and interactive experiences

Our growing collection of free games and interactive experiences explores creativity, experimentation, problem solving, and learning through play.

**Play our games:** https://labyrinthfoundation.org/games

We casually refer to this growing collection as **Labyrinth Arcade**. A larger Labyrinth Arcade project is still in development and is not yet published.

### Website and infrastructure

We also use GitHub to build and maintain https://labyrinthfoundation.org and the tools and infrastructure that make our nonprofit programs available online.

## About the organization

Labyrinth Foundation Inc. is an independent nonprofit public charity. We are not a government agency, academic institution, political organization, or commercial software company.

Learn more about our mission and programs at https://labyrinthfoundation.org.
